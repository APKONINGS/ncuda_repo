# Image sources

All photographs were sourced from **Wikimedia Commons** under Creative Commons licences and downloaded on 4 October 2026. Each was resized for the web (max 2200 px, JPEG q80); two were also colour-graded (marked below). Adaptations of CC BY-SA works are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0.

**Accuracy note:** none of these photographs has been verified as taken in Ngwo itself. Each is captioned on the site with where it *was* taken: Momo Division (the division Ngwo belongs to) or, more broadly, the Northwest Region. The site never labels them as Ngwo. Photographs of Ngwo contributed by NCUDA members should replace them over time. Get permission from the people pictured before publishing.

Photographs of **Ngwo, Enugu State (Nigeria)**, a different community with the same name, were deliberately excluded.

## Non-photographic assets

`textures/grain.svg`, the logo mark (`components/brand/Logo.tsx`) and all motifs (`components/brand/Motifs.tsx`) are **original** works created for this project. The motifs draw loosely on the geometric design language of the Grassfields. They do not reproduce any specific Ngwo textile, Ndop cloth or toghu embroidery pattern.
