NCUDA Member Portal · Organisation blueprint

How NCUDA runs on the member portal

One private portal for the whole association: who belongs where, who holds which post, how members join and stay active, and how chapters and committees do their work. There is no public sign-up. Every account is created by an officer.

2 · Setting up

From one President General to a working organisation

Everyone starts inactive, the President General included, and becomes active once they belong to a chapter and their registration is recorded. Until then, officers can still do the setup work below.

  1. President GeneralCreates a region with its Regional Coordinator

    Creates the coordinator's account at the same time, with a temporary password.

  2. Regional CoordinatorCreates a chapter with its President and Financial Secretary

    A chapter cannot exist without both, because the Financial Secretary is the one who registers members.

  3. Financial SecretarySets the chapter's registration fee

    One amount for everyone in that chapter. Different chapters may charge different amounts.

  4. President General and CoordinatorJoin a chapter

    They pick their chapter from a list; a coordinator only from their own region. Once a chapter they could join exists, joining comes first: until they do, they cannot set up anything else.

  5. Financial SecretaryCollects registration on the Registration page

    One click per person, starting with their own. Each person becomes active as soon as it is recorded.

  6. Chapter officersComplete the team and register members

    The President appoints the remaining officers; the Financial Secretary registers new members, who pay the fee and are active at once.

3 · A member's journey

Joining, staying active, moving

Registered

By their chapter's Financial Secretary, who records the fee.

→
Active

Takes part in meetings, sees documents, pays towards projects.

→
Yearly renewal

The President General starts it; everyone is inactive until they pay again.

→
Active again

As soon as the year's registration is recorded.

Moving chapter

Transfers

  • Done by a Regional Coordinator (within their region) or the President General.
  • Any unpaid fine must be paid first.
  • A chapter post must be ended first; the member's history moves with them.
Suspension

Suspending and reinstating

  • Always with a written reason, recorded in the audit log.
  • A suspended member cannot sign in; their records are kept.
  • Reinstating restores their access exactly as it was.
Posts

Appointing officers

  • One person holds one post at a time; the current post is ended before a new one starts.
  • Vice Presidents hold all of their President's powers.
  • Every appointment and ending is recorded with who did it.
4 · Meetings and the work that follows

Meetings lead to decisions, and decisions lead to action

Meetings are held at three levels. The Secretary of each level runs the paperwork; at regional level, which has no Secretary, the Regional Coordinator does.

Decisions and actions

Public Relations

Documents

  • Uploaded once and shared with a chapter, a region, the whole organisation or chosen offices.
  • A meeting's papers are added by its Secretary and seen only by the people the meeting is for.
  • Downloads are checked and recorded.
Notifications

Messages

  • Officers message their area or a group, such as all Chapter Presidents, with attachments.
  • Messages can be forwarded unchanged, with a note.
  • Meeting notices, reminders and assigned actions arrive here automatically.
Records

On paper and in spreadsheets

  • Minutes, attendance registers, member lists and summaries print cleanly, with who printed them and when.
  • The same lists download as spreadsheets, holding only what that officer may already see. Every download is recorded.
5 · Committees

Small teams that carry out a project

  1. Project managerA New Project comes first

    Whole organisation (National Financial Secretary), a region (Regional Coordinator) or a chapter (its Financial Secretary), with its amounts. Its project manager holds its money: the National Financial Secretary in office for a national project, the region's Regional Coordinator in office for a regional one; a chapter project's money stays with the chapter's Financial Secretary.

  2. President General, Coordinator or Chapter PresidentCreates a committee for that project

    National committees by the President General, regional ones by the coordinator, chapter ones by the Chapter President. Several committees may share a project, for example by phase.

  3. The post in chargeAdds members and gives positions

    President, Vice President, Secretary, Financial Secretary, Treasurer. One active committee per person.

  4. CommitteeDoes the work

    Its own meetings, minutes and documents; reports to the post in charge, and receives project money from the project manager.

  5. The post in chargeDissolves it when the purpose is served

    With a written reason. Members are freed for other committees; records are kept.

A project does not need a committee. A committee is run by the post at its level (President General, Regional Coordinator or Chapter President), whoever holds it at the time. It is visible only to its members, that post, the project manager and the President General. The Regional Coordinator can also see, but not change, the chapter committees in their region. On the portal, committees are grouped into national, regional and chapter committees.

6 · Trust and privacy

How members are protected

  • No public sign-upOnly officers create accounts, after membership is complete at a chapter.
  • Members see their ownPersonal records are visible to the member and to the officers responsible for them.
  • Contacts for officers onlyOfficers see phone and email beside names; ordinary members see names only.
  • Everything is recordedThe audit log keeps who did what and when. It cannot be edited or deleted.
  • Records are never deletedMistakes are corrected openly, with a reason that stays in the history.
  • Each power has its placeAn officer acts only within their own chapter, region or the organisation.
  • Duties follow the postProjects, committees, meetings and their money belong to the post, not the person. When someone hands over a post, everything they ran goes to their successor.
  • Everyone belongs to a chapterOfficers at every level included. Nobody is active until they are in a chapter and registered there.
