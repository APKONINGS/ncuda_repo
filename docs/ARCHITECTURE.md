# Architecture

## Overview

```
app/
  (public)/            Home, About, Activities, Contact  (shared header/footer)
  member-portal/       Sign-in + forgot password (no sign-up route exists)
  dashboard/           The portal — one app, role-aware navigation and widgets
    actions.ts         Server Actions → lib/portal/commands
proxy.ts               Optimistic redirect for /dashboard without a session cookie
lib/
  auth/                Session cookie (HMAC), actor resolution, demo credentials
  permissions/         catalog.ts (permissions) · roles.ts (default config) · engine.ts (rules)
  portal/              queries.ts (reads) · commands.ts (writes) · visibility.ts · audit.ts
  demo/                Fictional fixtures + in-memory store (V1 data source)
  supabase/            server / browser / admin clients (admin = service role, server-only)
  content/             Public copy (facts vs placeholders) and the image registry
supabase/migrations/   Schema · RLS + RPCs + audit triggers · storage · role seed (generated)
tests/                 Vitest (TypeScript engine + commands) · tests/db (SQL + RLS in PGlite)
```

## Authorization model

**Authentication + Role + Permission + Scope.**

- **Scope** is `organization ⊃ region ⊃ chapter`. Every new chapter belongs to a region (diaspora chapters to diaspora regions). `region_id = null` remains possible only for chapters that existed before this rule (e.g. the President General's home chapter in the demo).
- **Chapter affiliation** (where a member belongs) and **role** (what authority they hold) are separate. Every member, including the President General, has exactly one open chapter row.
- **Roles** bundle granular permissions and are held at a scope. They are data (`roles`, `role_permissions`), not code. `lib/permissions/roles.ts` is only the default seed.
- **Central EXCO.** The President General leads with a Vice President General, National Secretary General, National Financial Secretary and Central Treasurer (organisation-level roles the President General appoints).
- **Regions.** Created by the President General (`CREATE_REGION`, RPC `create_region`) together with their Regional Coordinator — either an existing member from any chapter or a new person, whose account starts outside any chapter. A region without a coordinator gets one via `appoint_region_coordinator`. Coordinators need not belong to a chapter in their region (a new region has none).
- **Chapters.** A region's chapters are created by that region's Regional Coordinator (`CREATE_CHAPTER` held at region level, checked exactly — organisation-wide authority does not reach down here); nobody else creates chapters — not even the President General, who creates regions instead. Every chapter is created with its Chapter President **and its Financial Secretary** (who registers the chapter's members) — each an existing member (holding no post and owing no fines, who moves into the new chapter; their history is kept) or a new person, and two different people — plus optional other founding officers. The President General does not enrol ordinary members.
- **Own profile.** Every member edits their own name, email and phone (`update_my_profile`) and password on My profile. Changing the sign-in email needs the current password. A member's ID, membership number and creation details never change (trigger `guard_member_identity`).
- **Passwords (testing).** People created in the portal get a temporary password from whoever creates them and change it on My profile. **While testing, any non-empty password is accepted** (`passwordRule` in `lib/auth/demo-credentials.ts`); before hosting this must become a real password policy, handled by Supabase Auth.
- **Editing and moving.** The President General renames regions (`update_region`); a chapter's name and location are edited by its region's coordinator or the President General (`update_chapter`). Members move to another chapter — in the same region or not — with `TRANSFER_MEMBER` over both chapters (`transfer_member`): Regional Coordinators within their region, the President General anywhere. A chapter post is ended first, and any unpaid fine must be paid first (`owes_fines`; this also applies when an existing member is made President of a new chapter); the old chapter stays in the member's history. Someone not yet in a chapter can be placed in one, including by themselves.
- **Forms.** A refused submission keeps everything that was typed except the fields that were wrong, and moves the cursor to the first of those. Money boxes show thousand separators (`MoneyInput`) and submit plain numbers; the server also accepts "12,000".
- **Registration.** Everyone, the President General, coordinators and chapter officers included, must belong to a chapter and pay its registration fee there to be active (`registration_due()`).
  - **The fee:** each chapter's Financial Secretary sets it (`chapters.registration_fee`, `set_chapter_registration_fee`). It is one amount for everyone. It can be changed later; payments already recorded keep their amount.
  - **Recording payments:** the Financial Secretary records them, their own included, on the **Registration** page (the chapter's registration desk). It holds the chapter's fee, a one-click list of who still has to pay, the Register a new member form (which records the fee), and who has paid this year with receipts. Record a payment is for contributions and fines only. The President General's Registration page shows the whole organisation and the yearly renewal. A payment is always the full fee, once for the year in force (`contributions.registration_fee_year`).
  - **Which payments count:** before the first yearly renewal, any registration payment does. Once the President General starts the renewal for a year (`start_registration_renewal(year)`), a payment for that year is needed. Cancelling it brings back the previous period.
  - **People with no chapter:** the President General picks one from all chapters, a coordinator from their own region's chapters. The "Join a chapter" dropdown is on their Overview.
  - **While unpaid, offices keep only their setup powers** (`registration_allows()`): the President General creates regions and coordinators and manages registration; coordinators create chapters with their President and Financial Secretary; Financial Secretaries set the fee, register members and record registration payments only; anyone can join a chapter. Inactive members still sign in and see what to do.
  - **Everyone must belong to a chapter** (`must_join_chapter()`; `buildActor` in the app). Someone without one may set up only until a chapter they could join exists: the President General until any chapter exists, a Regional Coordinator until their region has one. From then on all they can do is join a chapter (their other powers return once they are in one, as setup powers until they register).
- **Committees.** A committee carries out one project. The President General creates national committees (`CREATE_COMMITTEE` at organisation level), Regional Coordinators regional ones (at their region) and Chapter Presidents chapter ones (at their chapter; their Vice President too); the project comes first. The National Financial Secretary (organisation), the Regional Coordinator (their region) or the chapter's Financial Secretary (the chapter) sets it up under Finances → Collections, with its purpose, amounts and dates (the end date is optional). The committee is then created for it (`create_committee`). The project must be for exactly the committee's scope, still current, and not a registration renewal. The committee takes its purpose from the project, and several committees may share one project (e.g. its phases or groups). Only the creator adds people (regional committees: from that region only; chapter committees: from that chapter only; the creator is a member only if they add themselves) and gives positions — President, Vice President, Secretary, Financial Secretary, Treasurer — or none (ordinary members). One active committee per person and one holder per position (unique indexes); committee positions are separate from chapter and regional posts. The committee President messages the creator (with attachments); the creator messages the committee. **Project money.** Members pay project contributions to their chapter's Financial Secretary. The **project manager** receives and holds a project's money: for a national project, the National Financial Secretary in office, whoever created it (`project_manager()`); for a regional project, the region's Regional Coordinator in office. A project needs no committee for its money to move. The chapter sends national and regional project money to the project manager and records it as a chapter expense (`expenses.sent_for_collection_id` / `sent_to`, which must be the project manager). A chapter cannot send more than it has collected for the project and not already sent. A chapter project's money stays in the chapter. The project manager (for a chapter project, the chapter's Financial Secretary) gives the project's committees their money (`expenses.committee_id`, `funds_committee()`); no more than the project has received (a chapter project: collected) and not yet given out. For a chapter project this is a chapter expense, shown in the chapter's books and Chapter Summary; otherwise it is shown only on the committee page, to those who can see its books. The project manager sees the project's committees and their books (`manages_project()`). Committees take no payments directly and record no spending: what matters is the result. **Duties follow the post, not the person.** A committee is run by the post at its level, whoever holds it at the time: the President General (and Vice President General), the region's Regional Coordinator, or the Chapter President (and Chapter Vice President) (`runs_committee()` / `committee_holders()`; `runsCommittee` in the app). That post adds members, gives positions, receives the committee President's messages and dissolves it with a reason; members are freed and the books close. Who created it is kept only as history. Likewise meetings are run and seen by the posts that hold meeting powers, not by whoever scheduled them; project money goes to the post in office (`project_manager()`); and a delegation counts only while whoever granted it still holds that power (`delegator_holds()`). The **audit log** follows the hierarchy by post too: an officer sees what happened in their area, but nothing done by someone whose post at the time was at a higher level (`audit_actor_level()` on the roles recorded with each entry). A Chapter President sees his chapter's chapter-level actions only, a Regional Coordinator her region's chapter and regional actions, the President General everything. A committee is visible only to its members, the post that runs it, its project manager and the President General, and a chapter committee also to the Regional Coordinator of the chapter's region, read only (`oversees_committee()`). The Committees page groups them into national, regional and chapter committees.
  - **Committee meetings, minutes and documents** (`committee_meetings`, `committee_attendance`, `committee_minutes`, `documents.committee_id`) mirror a chapter EXCO. The committee President or Secretary schedules meetings. The President, Vice President or Secretary records attendance and marks a meeting held or cancelled. The Secretary saves draft minutes, which only the Secretary and President can see, then publishes them; published minutes are final and the committee is notified. The President, Secretary or Financial Secretary shares documents, optionally for a meeting. Committee documents never appear in the general document library. Writes go through RPCs (`schedule_committee_meeting`, `set_committee_meeting_status`, `record_committee_attendance`, `save_committee_minutes`); the tables have read policies only.
  - **Committee message attachments stay with their message.** They are not committee documents. The committee page lists the messages each person sent or received, with their attachments. `send_committee_message` accepts only attachments the sender uploaded for the message.
- **Members without a chapter.** A newly created Regional Coordinator may not belong to any chapter yet. They act only through their office; they are listed organisation-wide (not under any chapter). **Testing only:** before the portal is hosted, every member — including the President General and Regional Coordinators — must belong to a chapter. That needs a way to place an existing member into a chapter, and then the rule enforced in the app and the database.
- **Projects (collections).** Named projects (`collections`) at organisation, region or chapter level, each with a purpose and what every **executive** (anyone holding an office) and every **ordinary member** is expected to pay. A project belongs to the body that sets it up: `MANAGE_COLLECTIONS` at *exactly* that scope (`canAtExactly` / `has_exact_permission`) — National Financial Secretary for the organisation, Regional Coordinator for their region, a chapter's Financial Secretary for the chapter.
- **Payment categories shown in the portal:** Contribution, Fine and Registration. A registration payment is stored as a `contribution` row marked with `registration_fee_year`. It is shown, filtered and summarised as its own category (`kindOf()`): "My Summary" filters a person's records by category, and each yearly summary card lists Fines, Contributions, Registration and Expenses. The summary is named after the widest scope the viewer sees: National Summary, Regional Summary or Chapter Summary.
- **Payments.** Two stored types only: `contribution` (always towards a project; its description is the project's purpose and its expected amount is the payer's rate, both set on the server / by trigger), `fine` (never part of a project; the officer gives the reason and the fine imposed; it is either paid in full when imposed, or recorded unpaid and later paid in full by one payment that points back with `settles_fine_id`). **There are no partial payments.** A contribution is always the payer's full amount due for the project, recorded once per member and project. The amount is set on the server and by trigger; the officer never types it. Receipt numbers (`RC-2026-00042`) are generated from `reference_counters`; clients cannot supply them. What each person still owes = expected − paid, per project and per fine; members see their own, officers with `VIEW_CONTRIBUTIONS` see who owes in their chapters, report viewers see totals only.
- **Contact details.** Wherever an officer (anyone holding a post, or a position on an active committee) sees a person, the person's phone and email are shown as tap-to-call and tap-to-email links. This covers finance records, who still owes, outstanding fines, registration, roles, committee members and creators, meeting attendance and responsibilities, members, regions and chapters, and who recorded, uploaded, created, scheduled, assigned or ended each record, document, project, committee, meeting or post. Ordinary members see names only (`lib/portal/contacts.ts`).
- **Expenses (money out).** `expenses` record spending from a chapter's, a region's or the central funds, by `RECORD_EXPENSE` at exactly that scope (chapter Financial Secretary, Regional Coordinator, National Financial Secretary). Visible up the hierarchy to anyone with `VIEW_CONTRIBUTIONS` or `VIEW_FINANCIAL_REPORTS` over that body; voided via `void_expense()`, never deleted; voucher numbers `PV-2026-00007`. The summary shows money in, money out and the balance per body.
- **One person, one post.** A member holds at most one active role (unique index `member_roles_one_active_post`, plus checks in `assign_role`, `create_chapter`, `appoint_region_coordinator` and the app). To move someone to another post, their current post is ended first; the Assign role form offers to end it on the spot, and post holders appear greyed out when choosing a coordinator or Chapter President.
- **Appointment chain.** `role_assignable_roles` lists which offices a role may fill: President General → Regional Coordinator and (for succession) Chapter President; Chapter President → every other office in their own chapter. The chapter's Financial Secretary registers its members (`CREATE_MEMBER`), since members pay their registration to them. Registering records the registration fee, which the Financial Secretary types in. It is the same for every new member, with no executive/member distinction, and is paid in full. It becomes a receipted contribution in the chapter's books (`contributions.registration_fee_year`, recorded only through `create_member`) and counts as that year's registration. Phone numbers take digits, spaces, hyphens and a leading + only, enforced both in the forms and in the database. Appointing is separate from the role's own permissions, so a Chapter President can appoint a Financial Secretary without being able to edit finances.
- **Messages.** Notifications target one member, a scope, or a *group*: holders of selected roles within a scope (`audience_role_keys`), e.g. every Chapter President. Senders need `MANAGE_NOTIFICATIONS` over that scope.
- **Forwarding.** A recipient with `MANAGE_NOTIFICATIONS` can forward a message *as received* to people within their own scope: subject and text are copied unchanged (enforced in the database by `valid_forward()`), the forwarder may add a separate note, the original sender and every forwarder are shown, and people who already have the message are not sent it again.
- **Documents (Public Relations).** Uploaders with `UPLOAD_DOCUMENT` choose who can view each document: everyone in a scope, or only holders of chosen offices within it (`audience_role_keys`); those people are notified. Message attachments (`notification_attachments`) are visible exactly to the people who can see the message, including through forwards, which carry the original attachments only. One function, `document_visible_to_me()`, governs both the table and the private Storage bucket. Files: up to 10 MB, PDF/Office/text/images.
- **Suspension.** `SUSPEND_MEMBER` (Chapter President for their chapter; President General organisation-wide) via `set_member_status()`. Never yourself; office holders only by someone who appoints their office; a reason is required. A suspended member cannot sign in and holds no permissions, but their roles and history are kept, so reinstating restores access exactly. A trigger blocks status changes made any other way.
- **Delegation.** `authority_delegations` grants specific permissions at a scope for a period. You can only delegate what you hold, and never to yourself.
- **Hard rules:** no self-assignment, officers must belong to a chapter inside their role's scope, and VIEW is separate from RECORD/EDIT/VOID for finances.

The rules exist in three places that must stay in step:

1. `lib/permissions/engine.ts`: decides what the server renders and rejects bad requests early.
2. `supabase/migrations/…_functions_policies.sql`: `has_permission()`, the RLS policies and the RPCs. **This is the security boundary.**
3. The tests: `npm test` (92 engine/command tests) and `npm run test:db` (210 SQL/RLS checks against the real migrations).

Hiding a button is never authorization. Every page and Server Action calls `requireActor()` and re-checks permission and scope, and the database enforces the same rules again.

## History & accountability

- **Role history.** `member_roles` rows are ended, never deleted or rewritten (enforced by trigger).
- **Chapter history.** Transfers close the open row and open a new one. Chapter offices end automatically on transfer.
- **Finance.** There is no delete (payments or expenses). Corrections mean voiding (with a reason) and re-recording; in-place edits of amounts, descriptions, expected amounts and receipt numbers are blocked by trigger. A fine cannot be voided while a payment towards it stands.
- **Receipts.** Every payment recorded for a member (registration, contribution or fine, chapter or central ledger) sends the payer its receipt in their notifications: receipt number, amount, date, what it was for, where it was paid and who recorded it. A fine imposed to be paid later sends a notice of what is owed, and a voided record tells the payer it was cancelled, with the reason. In the app: `lib/portal/receipts.ts`; in the database: the `contribution_receipt()` trigger, so no client can skip or fake one. Project money a chapter sends gives the project manager a receipt the same way (`project_money_receipt()`), and a cancellation if the chapter voids it. Money the project manager gives a committee sends its receipt to the committee's Financial Secretary (otherwise its President, Vice President or Treasurer), outside the committee's message list.
- **Audit log.** It is append-only. Triggers record CREATE/UPDATE/VOID/ASSIGN_ROLE/REMOVE_ROLE/UPLOAD with old and new values, the actor, the actor's roles at the time and the chapter/region. `log_event()` records VIEW/DOWNLOAD/EXPORT/LOGIN/LOGOUT.

## Data source: demo now, Supabase next

V1 runs in **demo mode** (`NCUDA_AUTH_MODE=demo`). It uses fictional accounts and an in-memory store, so changes persist until the server restarts. Pages never read the store directly; they go through `lib/portal/queries.ts` and `commands.ts`, which take the `Actor`.

To move to Supabase:

1. Apply the migrations: `supabase db push` (or paste them into the SQL editor in order).
2. Create the first President General by hand: an auth user, a `members` row with `auth_user_id`, a `member_chapter_history` row and a `member_roles` row (`assigned_by = null`).
3. Implement `lib/portal/queries.ts` / `commands.ts` against `createSupabaseServerClient()`. Reads become plain `select`s (RLS filters them), and privileged writes call the RPCs (`assign_role`, `end_role`, `create_member`, `void_contribution`, …).
4. Replace demo sign-in with `supabase.auth.signInWithPassword`, and `getActor()` with a query for the member and their grants.
5. **Member invitations.** After `create_member` succeeds *as the officer*, call `auth.admin.inviteUserByEmail` with `lib/supabase/admin.ts` (server-only) and link `auth_user_id`.
6. **Documents.** Upload with a server-issued signed upload URL to the private `documents` bucket, and download with short-lived signed URLs after the same access check.
7. Disable public sign-ups in Supabase Auth settings. This is required, because there is no self-registration.

## Security checklist

- [x] There is no public registration route and no "sign up" copy.
- [x] The service-role key is only read in `lib/supabase/admin.ts` (`server-only`). It must never be given a `NEXT_PUBLIC_` name.
- [x] Session cookies are httpOnly, SameSite=Lax, Secure in production and HMAC-signed. Production refuses to start demo sessions without `SESSION_SECRET`.
- [x] Sign-in uses generic error messages and is throttled (5 failures per 15 min per account).
- [x] Records that are not visible return "not found", so their existence isn't leaked.
- [x] Financial views are audited; downloads are permission-checked and audited.
- [ ] Before production: enable MFA for officers, set CSP headers, and use a shared rate-limit store (the current throttle is per-process).

## Out of scope for V1 (designed to fit later)

Online payments, SMS/WhatsApp, a PWA or mobile app, calendar sync, advanced analytics, development-project management, public news/events CMS, and meeting scheduling and minute-writing UIs (the permissions and tables already exist).
- **Regional Coordinators and money.** Read-only access to their region's chapters (`VIEW_CONTRIBUTIONS` at region level): Chapter records, who still owes, outstanding fines, and a read-only Registration page for the region (who has and has not paid). Coordinators also create regional projects (`MANAGE_COLLECTIONS`) and record the region's own spending. They never record or void members' payments; that stays with each chapter's Financial Secretary.
- **Vice Presidents** hold all of their President's powers, appointing included (`deputise()` in `lib/permissions/roles.ts`). This covers the Vice President General, the Chapter Vice President and a committee's Vice President. A Vice President cannot appoint to their own post or to the President's.
- **Member order:** chapter executives by rank (President, Vice President, Secretary, Vice Secretary, Financial Secretary, Treasurer, Socials), then Regional Coordinators, then national executives by rank, then ordinary members A–Z (`memberStanding()`).
- **Projects** (Finances → Contributions) are grouped by scope (Whole organisation, Regional, Chapter), newest first. Each shows the post that set it up, as held at that time (e.g. "Buea Financial Secretary", `postAt()`).
- **Meetings** (`lib/portal/meetings.ts`). Only the Chapter Secretary (their chapter), the Regional Coordinator (their region) and the National Secretary General (national) schedule meetings, with `CREATE_MEETING` exactly at that scope.
  - **Who a meeting is for** depends on its level. A chapter meeting is for all its members or its executives. A regional meeting is for all members of the region or chosen executives. A national meeting is for all members, or a chosen mix of executives, whole regions and whole chapters; every member of a chosen region or chapter is called (`meeting_invitees`, `meeting_invite_groups`).
  - **Visibility:** only those people, the scheduler and the officers who run meetings there can see it (`meeting_visible()`). Everyone it is for is notified when it is scheduled, changed or cancelled, and when minutes are published.
  - **Running it:** officers with `EDIT_MEETING` (including Presidents) change it, cancel it with a reason, or mark it held. Attendance is taken for the people the meeting is for.
  - **Minutes:** the Secretary writes and publishes them (the Vice Secretary may draft); the Regional Coordinator does so for regional meetings. Published minutes are final.
  - **Decisions** are recorded by the Secretary (the Regional Coordinator for regional meetings, the National Secretary General nationally) and may carry an action with a person responsible and a due date; that person is notified. Only the Secretary marks an action complete, not the person responsible or a President (`complete_action_item()`). Presidents still follow open actions on their overview.
  - **Add to calendar:** an upcoming meeting's page offers Google Calendar and Outlook links (the event opens filled in) and a calendar file (`.ics`) for Apple Calendar, the Outlook app and phones. The file is served by `/dashboard/meetings/[id]/calendar` only to those who can see the meeting, with a reminder an hour before; a meeting without an end time is given two hours (`lib/calendar.ts`). Adding is one-off: later changes are not pushed to calendars.
  - **Reminders:** everyone a meeting is for is reminded 24 hours before and at 07:00 WAT on the day, each once (only the morning one when both fall due together). Reminders already due when a meeting is scheduled or moved are skipped, and a new time starts them over. Testing mode sends them as portal pages load; on Supabase, `send_meeting_reminders()` runs every 15 minutes through pg_cron (enable the extension before running the migrations; the job is then scheduled automatically). Nobody else can call it or mark reminders sent.
  - **Attendance register** (Attendance page): past chapter, regional and national meetings, grouped by level, for those who take attendance (Secretaries, Regional Coordinators, the National Secretary General) or review it (officers who see the members there). Meetings not yet recorded are flagged.

## Spreadsheets and printing

- **Download spreadsheet** (CSV, opens in Excel and Google Sheets) on Members, Chapter records (with the filters in use), Expenses, Registration, the attendance register and each meeting's attendance. Each file holds exactly what the person can already see on that page, built from the same permission-checked queries (`lib/portal/exports.ts`, served by `/dashboard/export/[kind]`), and each download is written to the audit log. Cells that would run as spreadsheet formulas are written as text.
- **Print** on the same pages, the Chapter/Regional/National Summary and meeting pages (minutes, decisions and attendance). Printouts leave out the menus, buttons and forms, and carry a line saying who printed them and when.
