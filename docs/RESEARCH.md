# Cultural research notes

This file records what was researched before designing the site, what was verified, and what was deliberately avoided. Public copy in `lib/content/site.ts` (the `facts` object) uses only the verified items below.

## Deliberately NOT used

- **Ngwo, Enugu State, Nigeria.** This is a different community. Its Odo festival, "Gwo Gwo Ngwo" dance and pine-forest photos are unrelated and were excluded.
- **"Nchenfem Cultural & Development Association (NCUDA)".** This is a different organisation that shares the acronym.
- **Population figures** (25,000 in Ntongieh 2015, which cites "NCUDA 2014"; 50,000 elsewhere). The sources conflict and the figures are not current, so no statistics appear on the site.
- **Language-endangerment findings.** Sensitive. Leave it to NCUDA to decide whether to discuss.
- **The palace name**, festival names, dances, regalia and titles specific to Ngwo. None could be confirmed from a reliable source, so none are named.
- **Ndop / toghu patterns as decoration.** The embroidered toghu (atoghu) is worn across the Northwest Region. Photographs of it are captioned as regional, and no textile pattern is reproduced.
